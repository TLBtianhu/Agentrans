# Artifact visual review

Use this checklist after generating or revising an Artifact. A correct interaction is necessary but not sufficient for a good explanation.

## Required views

- Desktop: 1440×960, light and dark.
- Mobile: 390×844, light and dark.
- Main interaction completed: prediction, launch, parameter change, or confirmation.
- Reduced-motion run: result remains understandable without transitions.

## Inspect with `read_image`

Look for these defects before delivery:

- Text or controls clipped by a card, canvas, or viewport.
- Weak hierarchy: every region has equal visual weight, too many accents, or a giant title with no experiment.
- Crowded controls, tiny touch targets, unexplained icons, or unreadable muted text.
- Result hidden below the fold or disconnected from the action that produced it.
- Excess decoration, moving backgrounds, or animation that distracts from the model.
- A plain white card with buttons where a designed lab was requested.

If any defect remains, revise the template and capture the same view again. Do not describe an Artifact as polished from source alone.
